A little about us

Engineers by profession.
Malayalees by heart.

MEANT brings together Malayalee engineering professionals, families, and friends in North Texas. We create opportunities to connect across careers and generations while celebrating the Kerala heritage we carry with us.

From STEM education and professional exchange to Onam, Vishu and community service, our strength comes from the people who participate, volunteer, and make each experience possible.
