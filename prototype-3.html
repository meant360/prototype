History & mission

Fifteen families. Five pillars. 1997.

MEANT — the Malayalee Engineers' Association of North Texas — was founded in November 1997 by a small group of engineers from Kerala who saw a gap: no socio-professional forum existed to connect Indian engineers with Malayalee roots settling into North Texas.

From fewer than fifteen member families, the chapter has grown to more than two hundred, becoming — by its own charter — "the most active socio-professional forum for Indian engineers with Kerala/Malayalee links" in the region.

Primary mission

Provide an infrastructure for the professional and personal growth of present and future Indian engineers with Kerala/Malayalee links.

Secondary mission

Foster community involvement and build social and cultural bonds among members and families through regular, welcoming interaction.
