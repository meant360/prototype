A little about us

More than a network.
A place to belong.

MEANT brings together Malayalee engineering professionals, families, and friends in North Texas. We create opportunities to connect across careers and generations while celebrating our shared heritage.

From educational initiatives and professional exchange to cultural celebrations and community service, our strength comes from the people who participate, volunteer, and make each experience possible.
